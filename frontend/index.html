Analytics

Understanding RFM Analysis

RFM analysis helps understand customer purchasing behaviour using three measures:

Recency (R)

How recently a customer made a purchase. A lower recency value means a more recent purchase.

Frequency (F)

How many distinct invoices or purchases a customer has made.

Monetary (M)

The total amount spent by a customer.

Clustering Method

The dashboard uses K-Means clustering with four clusters to group customers according to their RFM behaviour.

Workflow: Data preprocessing → RFM calculation → Feature scaling → K-Means clustering → Customer segments.
